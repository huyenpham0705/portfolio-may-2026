Project Introduction

This is our group assignment for Experience Design 3. The project focuses on conducting Primary and Secondary Research (the first diamond of the Double Diamond framework) on screen habits of children aged 0–6.

Briefing

HMW Question

Our HMW question is "How might we guide parents to make sure that the content their kids consume benefits their development based on their age (0-6)?"

Research Approach

We applied the Double Diamond framework, focusing specifically on the first diamond - Designing the Right Thing. Our secondary research examined recommended screen‑time guidelines for young children, the relationship between screen exposure and cognitive development, the impact of different content types on early childhood, parents’ mental health, and existing parental‑control solutions.

For primary research, we conducted surveys, interviews, and a cultural probe to capture real behaviours and attitudes within families. We used AI‑assisted analysis to synthesize interview insights and identify recurring patterns that informed our problem framing and early concept direction.
