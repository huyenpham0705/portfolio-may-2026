A team-player

In a team, I’m both a Shaper and a Completer. I believe a structured plan is essential for consistently meeting goals, and I always do a thorough final check before submitting or sending any work. I’m receptive to feedback and genuinely enjoy hearing different perspectives. I always strive to be a more understanding and supportive teammate.

My Process

After learning about user experience, I began applying the Double Diamond framework to my design process. At each stage, I dive as deeply into research as possible and explore a wide range of options. This allows me to narrow down and select the most suitable solution based on the project goals and requirements.

My Inspiration

Culture is my biggest source of inspiration, and before I can truly appreciate other cultures, I believe it’s important to understand and value my own. I explored elements of Vietnamese culture - its history, religion, literature, and music - and drew deeply from these influences throughout my work.
