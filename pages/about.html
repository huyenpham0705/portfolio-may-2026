My Story

My journey into design started with a fascination for how colors and shapes could influence human emotion. What began as a hobby in graphic design evolved into a deep-rooted passion for UX/UI—solving complex problems by putting the human experience at the center of every pixel.

My Process

I don't start with a canvas; I start with a question. Whether I'm building a brand identity or a mobile app, my process involves deep research, messy sketching, and rigorous testing. I thrive in collaborative environments where feedback is honest and the goal is always the best possible user outcome.

Beyond the Screen

When I'm not pushing pixels, you'll likely find me capturing urban landscapes through a 35mm lens, exploring local coffee shops, or traveling to find new cultural inspirations. I believe a well-traveled mind is a designer’s best asset.

What's Next

I am currently looking for opportunities to join a creative team where I can contribute to high-impact digital products and continue growing in the fields of Motion Design and UX Strategy.
