Project Introduction

For this project, I am designing an interactive storytelling website that aims to debunk a common misconception about Marie Antoinette.

Briefing

The story of Marie Antoinette

It is a common misconception that Marie Antoinette was indifferent to the suffering of her people and said, “Let them eat cake” during a time of famine. In reality, there is no historical evidence supporting this claim. This interactive website presents the true story and debunks the myth through engaging storytelling.

The focus of this project

In this project, I focused on creating a visually captivating and interactive experience that educates users about the true story of Marie Antoinette. The website is designed around a card‑themed interface, inspired by her fondness for card games, with each card revealing a different aspect of her life and historical context. Alongside the interactive elements, I emphasized clean, elegant illustrations to enhance the overall storytelling.
